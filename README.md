# HUMAN DELETER 3000

Welcome to **Human Deleter**, the world's most sophisticated (and incredibly petty) database for deciding the fate of your peers. Whether it's the guy who took the last slice of pizza or your neighbor who mows the lawn at 6 AM, this app lets you put them on a card and decide their destiny.

## What is this?
It’s a web app where you type in names, and they pop up as "Fate Cards." You then have the divine power to:
* **Eradicate:** Delete them from the DOM (and your life).
* **Spare:** Give them a 50% opacity "pass" because maybe they aren't *that* bad.

## Built With
* **HTML5:** The bones of the operation.
* **CSS3:** Making the UI look like a 2000s fever dream.
* **Vanilla JavaScript:** Using `document.getElementById` and `appendChild` like a total boss.

## How to use
1.  Open `index.html` in your browser.
2.  Enter the First Name and Last Name of your "target."
3.  Hit **SUBMIT INFO**.
4.  Decide if they get the **Eradicate** button (straight to the void) or the **Spare** button (they get a line-through their name and stay as a ghost).

## Disclaimer
* This does not actually delete humans. It only deletes `<div>` elements. 
* If you get an error saying `appendChild` is null, it’s probably because you didn't listen to the console.
